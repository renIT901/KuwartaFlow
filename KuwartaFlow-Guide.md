# KuwartaFlow — How to Use

KuwartaFlow is a money tracker that works the way you already jot things down in Notes.
You just type what you spent, one line at a time, and the app does all the adding up for
you. No forms, no tapping through menus.

Everything you type is saved automatically and stays on your phone.

---

## The basic idea

Type each thing you spent on its own line, like this:

```
40 pedicab cash
110 coffee gcash
200 massage cash
```

Each line is just: **how much**, **what it was**, and **how you paid**. That's it.
KuwartaFlow reads each line, adds up your total for the day, and neatly labels everything.

---

## The three tabs

At the bottom there are three tabs:

- **Log** — where you type your spending (your main screen)
- **Balances** — where you keep track of how much money you have in each account
- **History** — a list of all your past months

---

## Log tab — your daily spending

**Adding a day.** Tap **+ Add a day** to start a new day, then type your items into it,
one per line. Tap a date to change it.

**The total.** As you type, the day's total appears on each day card, and your total for
the whole month shows at the very top. You never have to add anything up yourself.

**Paying methods.** If you end a line with how you paid — `cash`, `gcash`, `gotyme`,
`maya` — KuwartaFlow recognizes it and gives it a colored label. Example:

```
80 uv cash
600 movie gotyme
```

**Tags (categories).** Add a `#` word anywhere on a line to group your spending, like
`#food`, `#transport`, or `#fun`. You can use a tag and a paying method together:

```
141 bfast and lunch cash #food
173 grab to robinsons gcash #transport
```

**Transfers and withdrawals don't count as spending.** When you just move your own money
around — sending from GoTyme to GCash, or withdrawing cash — type the word `transfer` or
`withdraw` in the line. KuwartaFlow will cross it out and leave it out of your spending
total, because you didn't actually spend it.

```
1000 gt transfer to gcash
600 withdraw gotyme to cash
```

---

## Monthly budget

At the top of the Log tab there's a **Monthly budget** box. Tap it and type the most you
want to spend in a month. A bar fills up as you spend:

- **Green** — you're comfortably under budget
- **Yellow** — you're getting close
- **Red** — you've gone over

Underneath, it tells you how much you have left, or how much you're over.

---

## Seeing where your money goes

**The little bars** under the total show how much you spent on each day of the month, so
you can spot your heavy days at a glance. Today's bar has an outline.

**By method / By tag.** Just below that, tap **By method** to see your totals split by
Cash, GCash, GoTyme, etc., or **By tag** to see them split by your categories like #food
and #transport.

---

## Switching months

Use the **‹** and **›** arrows at the top of the Log tab to move between months.

---

## Balances tab — what you actually have

This tab is for keeping track of how much money is sitting in each of your accounts
(GoTyme, GCash, Cash, Maya). Tap the amount next to each one and type your current
balance. The top shows your **total across all accounts**.

Under each account it also shows **how much you spent from it this month**, so you can
check it against what your real GCash or GoTyme app is telling you.

> Note: you type your balances yourself — the app doesn't connect to your real bank or
> e-wallet. Think of it as your own quick snapshot.

---

## History tab — your past months

This is a simple list of every month you've tracked, with the total you spent and how many
days you logged. Tap any month to jump straight to it.

This tab also has two buttons at the bottom:

- **Import backup** — to bring back data you saved earlier (see below)
- **Reset all data** — to erase everything and start fresh (it asks you to confirm first)

---

## Saving your data and backups

**It saves automatically.** Anytime you type something, it's saved. You can close the app
and come back later — your data will be there. It even works without internet.

Your data lives **only on this phone**. So it's a good idea to keep a backup now and then.

**To make a backup:** tap **Export** (top of the Log tab), choose **Backup**, then **Copy**.
Paste it somewhere safe — your Notes, an email to yourself, or a saved file.

**To restore a backup or move to a new phone:** go to **History → Import backup**, paste
your saved backup, and tap **Restore**.

---

## Exporting in other formats

The **Export** button also lets you get your spending out in two friendly formats:

- **Notes text** — looks just like a handwritten list (`june 1 - 441`, then your items).
  Great for pasting back into your iPhone Notes.
- **CSV** — opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets if you want to do more with it.

You can export just the current month or everything, and either **Copy** it or
**Download** it as a file.

---

## Quick cheat sheet

| You want to… | Type / do this |
|---|---|
| Log a purchase | `120 lunch gcash` |
| Add a category | add `#food`, `#transport`, etc. |
| Mark money you only moved | include the word `transfer` or `withdraw` |
| Set a spending limit | tap the **Monthly budget** box |
| See totals by category | tap **By tag** |
| Check your account money | go to the **Balances** tab |
| Save a backup | **Export → Backup → Copy** |

That's the whole app. Type naturally, and KuwartaFlow keeps the numbers straight for you.
